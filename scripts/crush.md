# Crush

I accidentally sent my teacher a text saying I had a crush on her. But somehow... she texted back.

"See me after class."

I immediately started panicking. I tried deleting the message. Too late. She had already read it.

The next morning, I walked into class. She was standing at the board. Then she looked directly at me.

"Someone here has a crush on me."

The entire class started laughing. I wanted to disappear.

Then she said... "Stand up."

I slowly stood up. She smiled. "Tell everyone what you told me yesterday."

I froze. Then my best friend shouted... "HE SAID HE WANTS TO MARRY YOU!"

The entire class exploded. I screamed. "THAT'S NOT WHAT I SAID!"

My teacher pulled out her phone. Then she played my voice message. My own voice came out of the speaker.

"I swear, she's the hottest teacher in this school."

I immediately ran toward the door. But she stopped me. "Wait."

She smiled. "I have something to tell you."

I slowly turned around. She looked at the entire class. "Actually... I have a boyfriend."

Everyone went silent. Then she pointed at the classroom door.

And my principal walked in. Holding a giant teddy bear.

He looked at her. Then at me. Still standing up. Face bright red.

And said... "So YOU'RE the one who texted my girlfriend?"

I didn't say anything.

He leaned down. And whispered... "Detention. Forever."
