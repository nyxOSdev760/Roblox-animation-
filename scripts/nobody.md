# Nobody

In this town, whatever someone calls you... becomes your name. Forever.

It goes on your birth certificate. Your driver's license. It floats right over your head so everybody can see it.

So people here are very careful with their words. Moms name their babies "Sunshine." Everybody calls the mailman "Sir." And you never, ever call anyone a name in traffic.

My uncle learned that the hard way. He honked at a guy once. The guy yelled "Idiot!" out the window. Twenty years later, his wedding cake said... "Congratulations, Karen and Idiot."

Then there's Chad.

Chad is the most popular kid in my school. And Chad names people.

The new kid sneezed in class once. Chad called him "Booger." Now the teacher takes attendance. "Booger?" "...Here."

The lunch lady gave Chad a small slice of pizza. Now her name tag says "Sloppy Joe."

The principal gave him detention. Now the sign on his office door says "Principal Pants."

And nobody has ever called Chad anything. Because the second you open your mouth... he names you first.

Then my little sister started at my school. Her name is Lily. She's eight. She draws me pictures of us as superheroes.

On her very first day, she bumped into Chad in the hallway... and spilled chocolate milk all over his shoes.

He looked down at her and said... "Who are you? Nobody."

The letters over her head faded away. "Lily" was gone. Now it just said... "Nobody."

At first it was kind of funny. Then the teacher skipped her at attendance. Her friends walked right past her. The lunch lady didn't give her a tray.

By Friday, our own dog didn't recognize her. My mom set the table for three.

Because when your name is Nobody... nobody notices you.

That night, Lily sat on my bed and whispered... "Do you still see me?"

"I see you," I said. And that's when I got an idea.

Monday was the school assembly. Chad was on stage, accepting "Most Popular." Every kid. Every teacher. Every parent.

I walked straight up to the microphone, holding my sister's hand.

"Everybody... this is my sister. She's SOMEBODY."

The letters over her head flickered.

"Say it with me. Somebody!"

The new kid stood up first. "She's Somebody!" Then Sloppy Joe. Then Principal Pants. The whole gym was chanting. "SOME-BODY! SOME-BODY!"

And over Lily's head, in big gold letters... "Somebody."

My mom started crying. The dog licked her face.

Chad grabbed the microphone. "Whatever. She's still a—"

But the whole gym was already looking at him. And all at once, three hundred people said it.

"Nobody."

And just like that... nobody ever noticed Chad again.

He's still at our school. Somewhere. We think.

Me? Everybody calls me "Hero" now. It's on my locker. It's on my backpack.

But then my mom found out I grabbed the microphone at a school assembly.

So this year, my birthday cake said... "Happy Birthday, Grounded."
