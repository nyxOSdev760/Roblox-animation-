# The Lie Counter

In this world, every time you lie, the number above your head goes up by one. Hit a hundred... and you disappear forever.

So everyone here is brutally honest. "Do you like my haircut?" "No." "How was dinner?" "Disgusting." My grandma just told the mailman he smells like feet.

Then there's me. I'm at ninety-nine.

Santa? Lie. My homework? Lie. The dog ate it? We don't even have a dog.

One more lie... and I'm gone.

So today, I didn't talk to anyone. Not one word.

Then my crush walked up to me in the hallway. Her number was zero. She's never lied in her whole life.

She looked me in the eyes and asked... "Do you like me?"

The whole hallway went silent. Everyone was staring at my number.

My hands were shaking. If I said no... I'd disappear.

So I told the truth. "Yes. I really like you."

The number didn't move. Ninety-nine.

The whole hallway started cheering.

She smiled and said... "I like you too."

And then her number went from zero... to one.
