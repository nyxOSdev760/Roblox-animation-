# Heavy

In this town, every time someone laughs at you, you float up a foot. And whoever laughed gets heavier.

Spill your milk? Somebody laughs. Up you go. Sneeze in class? Up again.

So everyone here is very, very nice. Nobody laughs at anybody. My uncle tripped at his own wedding... and spent the honeymoon on the ceiling.

Then there's Mayor Grubb. Every Saturday he does Roast Night in the town square, and he makes fun of everybody. Your haircut. Your car. Your mom's cooking.

He's so heavy his chair is made of steel. He leaves footprints in the sidewalk. And nobody has ever laughed at Mayor Grubb. Not once.

His favorite target is my little brother, Max. Max has a really squeaky voice. Every week the whole town laughs at him... and every week Max floats a little higher. He sleeps tied to his bed. I walk him to school like a balloon.

Then the doctor said... a few more feet, and the wind could take him.

So at the big Roast Night finale, I held on tight. Mayor Grubb pointed right at Max. "Say something, Squeaky!"

Everybody laughed. Because they were scared not to.

The string slipped out of my hand. Max started drifting up.

I jumped on stage and grabbed the microphone.

"Hey, everybody. Did you know the mayor's mom still cuts his sandwiches into little dinosaurs?"

Silence. Then somebody snorted.

Thunk. The mayor sank into the stage.

And then everybody got it. The lunch lady. The mailman. Grandma. The whole town laughed. Thunk. Thunk. Thunk.

Everyone he ever roasted came floating back down. Max landed right in my arms.

And Mayor Grubb sank through the stage... through the street... We still hear him sometimes. Down in the sewer. Yelling "Rawr."

Me? I laughed the hardest. I'm stuck in the sidewalk up to my knees.

Worth it.
