# Margins

For three weeks, a mystery girl wrote back to me in the margins of the same library book.

I never knew who she was. So I told her everything.

I wrote that I practiced saying "hey" in the mirror. That I was way too scared to talk to girls. And that there was a girl two seats ahead of me who stole my pencil every single day... and I never said a word.

She wrote back. "Sounds like she likes you."

I wrote, "No way. She's the prettiest girl in school. I'd rather die than ask her out."

She wrote... "Dramatic."

So I got braver. I wrote her name. Zoe. In giant letters. With a heart. I even drew a tiny pencil.

The next day, the book was gone.

Then someone tapped my shoulder. It was Zoe. She was holding the book. Open to that exact page.

My soul left my body.

I tried to run, but she held up a pencil. My pencil. "You mean this one?"

"You... stole it."

"Every day," she said. "On purpose."

I stared at her. "Wait... you've been writing back?"

She smiled. "Dramatic."

Then she leaned in. "So... are you going to ask me out? Or do you still want to die?"
