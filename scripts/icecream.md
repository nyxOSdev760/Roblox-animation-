# Ice Cream

I told my entire school I was rich. I said my dad drives a Lamborghini.

Then my best friend said... "Prove it. Have him pick you up after school."

I froze. Everyone was staring at me. So I said... "Fine."

That night, I begged my dad to rent a fancy car. He laughed. Then he said, "Don't worry. I got you."

The next day, the whole school was waiting outside. Even my crush.

Then we heard music. Ice cream truck music.

The truck pulled up... right in front of me. The window slid open.

It was my dad.

He leaned out and yelled, "SON! YOU FORGOT YOUR LUNCHBOX!"

Everyone went silent. My crush covered her mouth. I wanted to disappear.

Then my best friend whispered... "Wait. Is the ice cream free?"

My dad smiled. "For his friends? Half price."

The entire school ran to the truck. The line went around the building.

By the end of the day, my dad made five hundred dollars.

He handed me fifty. "Your cut."

The next morning, everyone called me Ice Cream Boy.

And honestly? For the first time... I actually was rich.
