# Float

In this town, every time you laugh, you float. One laugh... one foot off the ground.

So everyone here is dead serious. Clowns are illegal. Babies wear ankle weights. My grandpa laughed at his own joke last Christmas... we had to get him off the roof with a ladder.

Then there's me. I've never laughed. Not once. Fourteen years. Zero feet.

My friends tried everything. Tickling. Funny faces. A whoopee cushion on the principal's chair. Nothing.

Then today at lunch, my best friend leaned in and whispered the worst dad joke ever.

"Why can't you trust stairs? They're always up to something."

I tried to hold it in. My face turned red. My cheeks were shaking.

And then... I snorted.

My feet left the ground. Then I laughed harder. Ten feet. Fifty. The whole cafeteria was screaming.

I floated past the roof... past the birds... past the clouds.

And up there, sitting on a cloud... was my dad.

The one who went to get milk eleven years ago.

He held up the carton and said... "Why can't you trust stairs?"
