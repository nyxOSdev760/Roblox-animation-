# Projector

I accidentally left my search history on the classroom projector.

Mr. Dunn asked me to pull up a video. I opened the browser. And the whole class saw my recent searches.

Silence. Then the first one appeared. "How to talk to Maya without sounding like a robot."

Maya sits in the front row. She turned around. Very slowly.

Then the next one scrolled in. "What time does Maya get to school."

Somebody whispered... "That's kind of sus."

"It's not what it looks like!" I said. It looked exactly like what it looked like.

Then my best friend Jake stood up. "Bro, scroll down. He practiced in the mirror. Twice."

"Where does Maya eat lunch." "Is it weird to stand near someone's locker." "Is Maya single."

The class screamed. I wanted to melt into the carpet.

Then Maya stood up and walked right over to me. Everyone went quiet.

"Is any of this true?"

I could barely breathe. "...Yes."

She smiled. "I'm single."

The whole class went... "OOOOOH."

I thought it was over. Until the speaker on the wall clicked on. It was the principal.

"Good morning, students. A reminder that the classroom projectors are connected to the cafeteria screens."

I looked at the screen. Every single search. In front of the entire school. For the last four minutes.

"Congratulations to both of you. And Mr. Dunn... that's still not the video."
