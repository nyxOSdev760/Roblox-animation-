# Matchmaker

In this town, if you're single at midnight, a machine picks your date. And nobody is allowed to say no.

So everyone panics at 11:59. My uncle got matched with a woman who hates uncles. The baker got matched with the health inspector. They're married now. The bread has never been worse.

Then there's Mayor Winters. He has never been matched. Because he's been engaged since 2009... to a cardboard cutout of himself.

And he abuses it. Anyone who complains at town hall wakes up matched with Gary. Gary lives with forty pigeons.

Then my mom asked the mayor one question at town hall.

So at midnight, the whole town gathered in the square. The mayor smiled and pressed the button. The machine hummed. "Match found. Linda... and Gary."

My mom went pale. Forty pigeons cooed.

So I stepped forward. "Mayor Winters. I read the town charter. An engagement expires after fifteen years. Yours expired Tuesday." I held up the paperwork.

His smile fell. The clock struck twelve. The machine beeped. "Mayor Winters is single. Searching for his perfect match."

The whole square went silent. Ding. "Match found. Mrs. Gable."

Mrs. Gable is ninety-four. She's the one he fined four hundred times for her lawn gnomes. She walked out in a wedding dress. "I've been waiting since 1962."

The mayor can't say no. It's his own rule.

And my mom's match got cancelled. But the cutout was single too. And I was the last single person in town.

So now I'm dating a cardboard mayor. It's flat. It never talks. Honestly? Best date I've ever had.
