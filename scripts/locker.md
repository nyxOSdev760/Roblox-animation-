# Locker

I accidentally hid in my crush's locker.

It started in gym class. Coach Reyes announced we were running the mile. Everyone groaned. I did not groan. I just quietly took a few steps backward... and then a few more... and then I ran. Not the mile. The other way.

I needed somewhere to hide. Fast. The hallway was empty, and there was one locker at the end with the door hanging open. I jumped inside and pulled it shut behind me.

It was tiny. It smelled like vanilla body spray and one very old banana. My knees were up against my chest. But it was perfect. Nobody would ever find me.

Then I heard footsteps. And a girl's voice.

"Wait... why is my locker unlocked?"

My blood turned to ice. I knew that voice. It was Chloe. The girl I'd had a crush on since September. The girl I had never once said a full sentence to.

I stopped breathing.

But she didn't open it. She just leaned against the door, right in front of me, so close I could hear her backpack zipper. And she started talking to her best friend, Mia.

"So? Who is it?" Mia asked.

"Who is what?"

"The guy you like. You've been smiling at your phone for a week."

Chloe laughed. "I'm not telling you."

"Is it Brandon?"

"Brandon eats glue."

"Okay. Tyler?"

"Tyler told me the Earth is flat."

I was loving this.

Then Chloe went quiet. "It's the guy in our class. The quiet one. His hair never stays down."

I slowly touched my hair. It was sticking straight up.

Then I hit my head on the shelf. THUNK.

"...Did you hear that?" Chloe asked.

"It's the building," Mia said. "This school is haunted."

I shut my eyes. Oh my gosh. Do not move. Do not move.

Then Coach Reyes's whistle echoed down the hallway. "Has anyone seen the kid who ran from the mile?"

"No, Coach," Chloe called.

"He's probably hiding somewhere," Mia whispered. "That's kind of his thing."

"Honestly," Chloe sighed, "I wish he'd just ask me out. I've been waiting since September. I even moved my seat next to his."

My heart did a backflip. She liked ME. Chloe liked ME. And I was sitting in her locker next to a banana.

I smiled so hard that I forgot to be quiet. And I sneezed.

Dead silence. Then the locker door slowly swung open.

Chloe looked down at me. Mia looked down at me.

"...Hi," I whispered.

Mia screamed. "HE WAS IN THERE THE WHOLE TIME?!"

I wanted to disappear forever. Then a shadow fell over all of us. It was Coach Reyes.

"THERE you are. Ten laps."

I closed my eyes. This was it. The worst day of my life.

But Chloe stepped in front of me. "Coach, I'll run with him."

Coach blinked. "Why?"

"Because..." She smiled. "He's my... helper."

Coach walked away, totally confused. Then Chloe turned back to me. "So... was that a yes?"

I slowly nodded.

She grinned. "Good." And she held out her hand. "Now can you get out? I need my gym shoes."
