# Locker

I accidentally hid in my crush's locker.

I was trying to escape the gym teacher. I jumped into the first empty locker I saw and pulled the door shut. Then I heard footsteps. And a girl's voice.

"Wait, why is my locker unlocked?"

My heart stopped. It was Chloe. The girl I'd liked since September.

She didn't open it. She just leaned against it, right in front of me, and started talking to her friend.

"Do you think he likes me?"

"Who?"

"The guy who stares at me in class."

I froze. Was that... me?

Her friend laughed. "He's the one who hides behind the library shelves every time you walk by."

"Yeah," Chloe sighed. "I wish he'd just ask me out."

I smiled so hard I forgot to be quiet. And I sneezed.

Silence. Then the locker door slowly opened. Chloe stared at me. Her friend stared at me.

"...Hi," I whispered.

Her friend screamed. "He was in there the WHOLE time?!"

I wanted to disappear. But Chloe just smiled. "So... was that a yes?"

I slowly nodded.

"Good." She held out her hand. "Now can you get out? I need my gym shoes."
