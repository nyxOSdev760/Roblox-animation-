# Intercom

I accidentally practiced asking out my crush... on the school intercom. For a whole week.

Mrs. Park said the front-office mic was broken. So every afternoon, while I waited for my ride, I whispered into it. "Hey Emma... you're really pretty... do you want to..." Forty-two times.

It was not broken.

On Friday, the whole school packed into the gym. The principal walked on stage. "Before we start, somebody has been sending us some very interesting audio."

My stomach dropped. He pressed play.

"Hey, Emma... you're really cool." A pause. "Cool? Who says cool?"

The gym exploded. Eight hundred kids. Laughing.

Then my best friend Jake stood up. "Bro, play the one where he does the robot voice!"

The speakers clicked. "Hello. Emma. I. Am. A. Human. Boy."

Everyone started chanting. "EM-MA! EM-MA!"

I tried to slide under my chair. Then Emma stood up and walked straight toward me. The whole gym went silent.

"Was any of that for me?"

My voice cracked. "...Yes."

She smiled. "I'm single."

The gym went "OOOOOH."

Then the principal leaned into the microphone. "Emma, please say yes. I have heard this forty-two times, and I cannot hear 'cool' again."
